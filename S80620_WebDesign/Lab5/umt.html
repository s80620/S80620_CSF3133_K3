Welcome to UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university is known for its focus on marine science, fisheries, oceanography, and environmental studies.

About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu has developed from an institution focused on fisheries and marine science into a university offering various academic programmes. It contributes to education, research, and innovation in Malaysia.

The university provides opportunities for students to develop knowledge and practical skills through academic learning, research, and community activities. Its location near the South China Sea supports studies related to marine and coastal environments.
